Why Suder

Lawyers who have sat on every side of the table.

Our founder spent nearly four years as the City of Cincinnati's Chief Counsel for Land Use and Planning, wrote its land development code, and now rewrites zoning codes for cities across the country. Our transactional lawyers were partners at global and national firms. Our eminent domain counsel is a certified planner and former civil engineering project manager.

That mix of government, big-firm, and planning experience is why Chambers USA has ranked Sean Suder in Band 1 for Ohio zoning and land use every year since 2019, why Best Lawyers named him Cincinnati's Land Use and Zoning "Lawyer of the Year" for 2026, and why Am Law firms call Suder when their clients' matters land in Ohio.

Infill development

Hyde Park Square rezoning

Cincinnati · 2024–2025

Lead counsel for the successful effort to rezone a portion of Hyde Park Square for a $150,000,000 mixed-use development with apartments, a boutique hotel, and underground parking. A high-profile, contested neighborhood approval.
Historic preservation

Saving the Washington Park bell tower

Over-the-Rhine · 2022–2023

Lead counsel for the successful effort to save the 150-year-old church bell tower on Washington Park, including multiple emergency court orders, personally argued and won by J.P. Burleigh, that halted demolition in the Over-the-Rhine Historic District.
Historic adaptive reuse

Crosley Building adaptive reuse

Camp Washington · 2020–2021

Lead land use counsel for the $50 million conversion of the former Crosley Radio Corp. headquarters into 175 to 250 apartments, with a focus on providing units to artists.
Published opinion

Taxpayer standing: Cincinnati ex rel. Miller v. Cincinnati

2024-Ohio-4805 (1st Dist.)

Representing the affordable housing developer, Over-the-Rhine Community Housing, Suder defeated a taxpayer suit attacking the City ordinance that approved the project. The First District vacated the judgment and ordered the case dismissed for lack of standing.
Sale-leaseback financing

Grocery-anchored downtown tower

Downtown Cincinnati · 2018

Lead transactional counsel on the sale-leaseback financing for a new 18-story downtown apartment tower anchored by a new multi-level Kroger store.
Condominium structuring

W Austin Hotel & Residences condominium

Austin, Texas

Josh Bernstein prepared the condominium documents for the W Austin Hotel & Residences, the mixed-use tower combining a hotel, private residences, and commercial space, using the condominium regime to allocate ownership, control, and cost among very different uses in one building.
